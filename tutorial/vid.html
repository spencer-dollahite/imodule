Pick your platform (setup half)

Pick the native path. x86-64 PC or Intel Mac → VirtualBox (or VMware); Apple-Silicon Mac → UTM. An Intel Mac runs the x86_64 appliance natively. Emulation (a non-native appliance, e.g. x86_64 on Apple Silicon) is a last resort — slow, only if native won't work. Have ready: the OneDrive link, your VM login, your cs3670 key.
